# Kingswood Villas, Chestwood Court Tower 1, Flat G

Historical research notes below. The completed six-tower registration, current coordinates, facade normals and estimated-height model supersede these notes: see [KINGSWOOD-FIRST-TOWERS.md](KINGSWOOD-FIRST-TOWERS.md) and [the visual report](kingswood-report.html).

Checked 9 September 2026.

Official LandsD Building feature: `1797235344T20050430`, name `Chestwood Court Block 1` / 翠湖居第一座. Query [the official layer](https://portal.csdi.gov.hk/server/rest/services/common/landsd_rcd_1637211194312_35158/MapServer/0) with `where=BuildingCSUID='1797235344T20050430'`, `outFields=*`, `outSR=4326`, `f=pjson`. Raw feature retained in `data/chestwood-tower-1-official-footprint.json`.

The feature gives BaseHeight 10.5, TopHeight 102.9 and Storeys 34. These are building attributes, not verified elevations of numbered residential floors. Dividing the overall height by the storey count would not establish a correct window height and has not been done.

[28Hse estate page](https://www.28hse.com/en/estate/detail/kingswood-villas-chestwood-24070) publishes a [plan for towers 1, 3, 5; floors 1–32](https://i1.28hse.com/estate_data/108/4390/FLOOR/4390_20231121230158764526_large.jpg). This is a secondary source, not an official developer or government plan. It identifies G and its living-room opening but has no north arrow. Its footer also has an address inconsistent with the estate page, so it requires corroboration.

Imported: estate, Tower 1, floors 1–32 (review), Flat G, pending living-room window. No geographic orientation was inferred from the page's upward direction. Camera coordinates, heading and reference altitude remain null. The owner-community website found through references, chestwoodcourt.com, was unavailable during research.
