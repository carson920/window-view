WINDOW RECOGNITION
- Trace the actual source-plan window/glazing representation.
- Never derive a window from room centre, guessed facade, LandsD footprint, or symmetry alone.
- Read `window-samples/window-pattern-presets.json` and all user-provided `window-samples/window-pattern-samples.json` items. These samples are approved semantic references; multiple samples may exist. Do not copy their coordinates.
- City One contains projected and corner bay windows. If a room clearly terminates in a repeated bay-window module but a separate thin sill line is not visible at source resolution, do NOT automatically mark it unresolved. Classify the pattern using the approved samples/presets and trace the primary outward viewing face as `bay-window-face` or `corner-bay-face`.
- For multi-face bays, preserve distinct glazing faces only when they represent materially different view directions. Never trace the entire bay/ledge outline as one window.
- Respect negative samples. AC platforms, service ledges, and generic structural projections are not windows.

LABELS / REVIEW IMAGES
